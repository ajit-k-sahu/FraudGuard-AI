AI / ML Architecture

Engineered for Imbalanced Financial Data

Financial fraud datasets suffer from extreme class imbalance (typically less than 4% fraud). FraudGuard -AI evaluates models specifically on Precision-Recall Area Under the Curve (PR-AUC) and Recall to minimize false negatives without drowning fraud teams in false alarms.

  • Selected Active Model: {{ ml_meta.selected_model or 'Random Forest Classifier' }}
  • Reference Dataset: PaySim Mobile Money Benchmark (15,000 transactions)
  • Oversampling Policy: Training-set isolated transformations to prevent data leakage
  • Persistence: Joblib serialized scikit-learn pipeline containing both preprocessors and estimators
